05 / TROUBLESHOOTING YOUR LUNCH

Small fixes. Big difference.

01

My bread gets soggy.

Dry washed leaves and blot tomato slices. Keep juicy tomato between fillings rather than directly against the bread. If packing for later, a thin butter layer can help limit moisture absorption.

02

The filling keeps escaping.

Try thinner, even layers instead of a tall central pile. Keep a small border around the edge, close gently, and cut with a sawing motion rather than pushing straight down.

03

It tastes a little flat.

For this recipe, try a little mustard with the mayonnaise, a pinch of pepper on the tomato, or swap some tomato for well-drained pickle slices. Change one thing and take another bite.
